How a study session turns into a drink

Five steps, in the order they happen. No points to decode, no tier chart to read.

1

Block your distracting apps

You pick the apps. Mr. Tapioca shields them for the length of the session using Apple's Screen Time, so tapping the one you always tap gets you him instead of the feed. He re-asserts the shield every few minutes while you focus, so it does not quietly fall off halfway through.

The shield comes off when the session finishes and you come back to the app. If the phone is in your bag past the end time, your apps stay blocked until you open Mr. Tapioca again, and there is no notification to tell you the cup is done. Reopening him takes a second and clears it. We would rather write that down than let you meet it cold. iPhone only. Screen Time is an Apple system feature, so only the native app can use it. In a browser he can still time the session and brew the drink, but he cannot lock anything, and this page will not pretend otherwise.
2

Finish the focus session

Set a cup anywhere from 15 minutes to 4 hours, or aim at your daily goal. He mixes the whole time you do. Finish and the drink goes on your shelf with its size and its minutes on it. Long cups are resumable, so standing up for a snack does not cost you the drink.

3

Watch the reward fill up

Every finished drink adds its minutes to your running total, and the app tells you how much focus is left until the next reward. It is cumulative, added up across as many sittings as it takes. Nobody has to sit still for four hours in one go to get there.

4

Redeem it at a partner boba shop

At 240 minutes of focus you are holding a reward. Open the Boba Map, tap the starred shop, and show the card at the counter. The card shows the shop, the perk in that shop's own words, and a live ticking clock, which is there so the card reads as something happening now rather than an old screenshot.

Read the shop name on the card before you show it. It is the one on the list below or it is nothing. Two partner shops exist today, both in Ithaca, New York, both named further down this page. There is no national network behind this and we are not going to imply one.
5

Keep a streak, dress him up

Focus days build a streak. Focus minutes earn pearls, and pearls buy hats, tea bases, toppings and whole new shop backgrounds. There are 14 looks for him, from a graduation cap to a dragon, most of them earned and a few sold as $1.99 in-app purchases. This whole layer works anywhere on earth, with or without a partner shop nearby.

Not near Ithaca?

Then be straight with yourself about what you are downloading. Everything except the counter discount works wherever you are.

  • The timer, the app blocking, the drinks, the pearls, the streaks, the skins and the shop backgrounds all work anywhere.
  • The Boba Map finds real bubble tea shops near you anywhere in the world. Only the two shops named above have a deal with us.
  • The real discount is two shops in one college town. If yours should be on the list, tell them about us, or tell us about them.
How the star works, since it matters to a business: the app matches a star to a map pin by location and name. On a block with several shops close together it can land on the one next door. The card always names the shop it is for. If that name is not the one over the door, there is no deal there, and please do not ask them for one.

What works where

One app, two places to run it, one honest difference between them.

  • iPhone. Everything, including real app blocking through Screen Time. This is the version to get.
  • Any browser. The same app, installable to your home screen, works offline. Everything except the app blocking.
  • No web browser can block another app. That is a limit of the web itself, not a corner we cut.

He earns the outfits too

Focus time pays pearls, roughly one every 15 minutes with the shield up, and half that on iPhone if you skip picking apps to block. Break games and the three daily quests pay a few on top. Pearls buy 9 of the 14 looks, 4 of the 9 shop backgrounds, and every tea base and topping. He starts with one look and one background already on, free.

The remaining 8 items, 4 looks and 4 backgrounds, are $1.99 in-app purchases instead. No amount of focus unlocks those. The shop shows the price on the item, so nobody saves up for something pearls were never going to buy. Everything cosmetic is optional either way, and none of it makes the timer go faster.
