# Publishing Mr. Tapioca Focus to the Chrome Web Store

Everything is prepared. Your part is register, upload, paste, submit.

## What's ready
- **Upload package:** `../mr-tapioca-focus.zip` (repo root)
- **Store text:** `LISTING.md` (name, summary, description, category, single purpose, permission justifications, privacy answers)
- **Screenshots (1280x800):** `screenshots/shot1.png`, `screenshots/shot2.png`
- **Privacy policy page (live):** https://mrtapioca.me/chrome.html#privacy

## Steps
1. Go to the Chrome Web Store developer dashboard: https://chrome.google.com/webstore/devconsole
2. Sign in with the Google account you want to own the extension.
3. First time only: agree to the terms and pay the **one-time $5 registration fee** (that $5 lets you publish up to 20 extensions, forever).
4. Click **New item** and upload `mr-tapioca-focus.zip`.
5. On the listing tab, paste the name, summary, description, and pick the category from `LISTING.md`.
6. Upload the two screenshots from `screenshots/`.
7. On the **Privacy practices** tab: answer "does not collect user data", paste each permission justification from `LISTING.md`, and set the privacy policy URL to https://mrtapioca.me/chrome.html#privacy
8. Click **Submit for review**.

## After you submit
Review usually takes a few days, though Google's own page warns it's running slower right now (a submission surge as of April 2026). Once it's approved and live, tell me the store URL and I'll swap the "Get the Chrome extension" button on mrtapioca.me from the zip download to a one-click "Add to Chrome" link.
