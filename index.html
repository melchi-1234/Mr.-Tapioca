iPhone feature

Lock out the apps that steal your focus

On iPhone, Mr. Tapioca uses Apple's own Screen Time to shield the apps you pick for as long as your session runs. Try to open one and you get a gentle wall instead of an endless scroll. Pause or finish, and everything unlocks right away. Blocking is optional, and every session works fine without it.

Brew & collect

Every focused hour brews a real drink

Pick a Custom Cup from 15 minutes up to 4 hours, or chase your daily Goal Cup. As you focus, your bubble tea fills up. Finish the session and it is added to your collection, along with pearls you can spend on new characters, tea bases, toppings, and shop backgrounds. Pearls come from focus time, about four an hour, so the reward is the work.

More to love

Built to make focusing feel cozy

Calm focus music

A dozen gentle, hand-picked tracks that loop softly in the background. Every one is properly licensed for the app.

Study Squad

Optional and anonymous. Share a code with friends and cheer each other on with a shared board of focus minutes, drinks and streaks.

Boba Map

Find real bubble tea shops near you, pulled from OpenStreetMap. A fun little nudge to go reward yourself after a long session.

Break mini-games

Earn a break and play Catch the Pearls, Boba Plinko or Cup Pong for a few extra pearls before the next round of focus.

Themes & backgrounds

Redecorate the tea room. Cozy daytime, calm night, a starry galaxy and more set the mood for your session.

Yours, privately

Your progress lives on your device. No account is required to focus, and there are no third-party trackers or ad networks.
