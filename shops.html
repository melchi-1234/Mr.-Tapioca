Students study for 4 hours. Then they come to you.

Mr. Tapioca is a free study timer for students. After 4 hours of focus, a student earns a perk at a partner boba shop, and the shop chooses what that perk is.

See how it works Current partner shops
What it is

A cozy focus timer with a boba mascot

A student starts a study session and a little tapioca pearl brews them a bubble tea while they work. On iPhone it can lock their most distracting apps with Apple's Screen Time until the session ends.

Their focus time adds up, and every 4 hours of it earns one perk at a partner shop. That is the part that brings them through your door.

Free, no ads On the App Store On the web at mrtapioca.me
How it works for your shop

From a study session to your counter

  1. A student studies 4 hours in total

    Across any number of sittings. One long afternoon or a week of short sessions both count.

  2. They walk in and show a counter card

    They open your shop on the app's Boba Map. The card shows your shop's name and the perk in big type.

  3. The clock on it ticks every second

    A screenshot freezes, so a still image is easy to spot. The live card keeps counting.

  4. Your barista honours the perk you chose

    The student taps "Use this reward" and the card is stamped Redeemed with the time, so it can't be shown twice.

What it costs you
Nothing.
  • Free to join. No fee, nothing to buy, and nothing to install. No scanner, no account, no code to type in.
  • You pick the perk. In your own words: a percentage off, a free topping, whatever works for your shop. The current partners each chose something different.
  • Leave any day. Ask, and your shop comes off the app that same day.
