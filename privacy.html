Mr. Tapioca Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026
Short version

Mr. Tapioca is a personal focus & study timer. We collect as little as possible. Almost everything stays on your device, we don't show ads, we don't use third-party trackers, and we never sell your data. The apps you choose to block are handled entirely on your iPhone by Apple's Screen Time system, and we never see them.

1. Information stored only on your device

The following never leaves your iPhone and is not sent to us:

  • Your progress, currency ("pearls"), streaks, achievements, drink collection, customizations, and settings. These live in your device's own storage.
  • Your blocked-app selections. When you choose apps to block, you pick them through Apple's own Screen Time picker. Apple stores that selection privately on your device; the app uses it only to apply a "shield" during a focus session. We never receive, transmit, or even see which apps you selected. Apple keeps that information private by design.

2. Study Squad and your anonymous account

The app creates an anonymous account on first launch (no email, password, or real name required). It exists so that Study Squad works the moment you want it, and so your friend code is stable. It is not linked to you, and it carries nothing until you use Study Squad. Only the following is stored on our backend, so that friends you share a code with can cheer you on:

  • A display name you choose.
  • An automatically generated friend code.
  • Basic focus stats: total focus minutes, drinks completed, and day streak. (There is a status field in the record, but the app currently always sends "idle". Friends do not see whether you are focusing right now.)

Only people who have your friend code can see these stats. You can delete this account at any time in Settings, which removes it from our backend.

3. Anonymous drink counter

When you finish a drink, the app sends one small anonymous row to our backend so we can answer "how many drinks has Mr. Tapioca brewed?". That row contains:

  • A random ID generated on this install, so separate devices can be counted. It is not your name, your email, or an advertising identifier, and it is not linked to anything else about you.
  • The drink size, the number of minutes, and whether you were on the iPhone app or the web app.

That is the whole row. It carries no name, no email, no location, and nothing about which apps you block. We use it as a counter and nothing else.

4. Partner rewards

On the iPhone app, partner rewards use the same anonymous account described above. To calculate eligibility and let a shop verify a one-time reward, our backend stores:

  • Your session start and end times, planned session length, whether the session came from the iPhone app, and the number of eligible focus minutes credited.
  • Rewards issued to the anonymous account, including their status and expiration date.
  • A short-lived redemption code, the partner shop selected, and whether the reward was accepted or refused. The code expires after five minutes and can be used once.

We use these records only to issue rewards, prevent the same reward being used twice, let a shop verify a code, and provide that shop with an anonymous redemption count. They contain no real name, email, precise location, advertising identifier, or list of blocked apps, and are not used for advertising or tracking across apps or websites.

5. Location (boba map)

If you open the in-app map of nearby boba shops, the app asks for your device location to find shops near you using OpenStreetMap data. Your location is used on your device to run that lookup and is not stored by us. You can decline; the rest of the app works fine.

6. What we do NOT collect

  • No real name, email, phone number, or contacts.
  • No advertising identifiers; the app contains no ads.
  • No third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. (The drink counter in section 3 is our own, goes only to our own backend, and is never used to track you across apps or sites.)
  • No location history, and no record of which apps you block.

7. Service providers

Study Squad accounts and partner reward records are stored using Supabase (a hosted database). Map data comes from OpenStreetMap. We do not share your information with anyone for advertising or marketing.

8. Children's privacy

Mr. Tapioca is a personal self-control tool and is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Data retention & deletion

Deleting the app removes all on-device data. If you used Study Squad, you can delete your anonymous cloud account in Settings → Delete account at any time. Your Study Squad profile, reward session records, rewards that have not been used, and active redemption codes are deleted with that account. Past anonymous redemption attempt records remain so we can keep an accurate count for partner shops and investigate problems at the counter. After account deletion, those records are no longer linked to an account or Study Squad profile.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
