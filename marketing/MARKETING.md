## The one-liner
A cozy focus timer where a little boba character brews your drink while you study, and your distracting apps stay locked until you're done.

## Why anyone cares (the hook angles)
- **The aesthetic.** Cozy, kawaii, study-with-me energy. This is the whole game on studygram/studytok.
- **The mechanic is actually new.** Your apps physically lock while a cute drink fills up. It's not another plain timer.
- **The reward is real.** Finish focus sessions, earn a drink, and it maps to actual discounts at real boba shops (once partners are on).
- **It's a pet you earn by focusing.** People love raising a little guy.

## Who we're talking to
College + high school students, exam-season crammers, the "romanticize studying" crowd, anyone who doom-scrolls instead of working. Aesthetic-productivity is the lane.

## Posting rhythm (don't overthink it)
- Post 4 to 5 short videos a week for the first month. Volume beats polish early.
- Reuse the same clip with different hook text. The hook is 90% of it.
- Reply to every comment for the first month, it pushes reach.
- Repost your best TikTok as an IG Reel and a YouTube Short same day. Same file, three platforms.
- Put a signup/waitlist or TestFlight link in bio and reference it as "link in bio" not in the caption text (links in caption kill reach).

## The built-in growth loop (most important part)
The **share card** (Share my drink, after every finished session) is the engine. Every time a user posts their card, that's a free, authentic ad with your branding baked in. Everything above is just priming the pump until users carry it themselves. Keep making the card prettier over time and it keeps paying off.
