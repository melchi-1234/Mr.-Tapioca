How it makes money
Free for students. Shops pay when it works.

Now · live

  • $1.99 one-time cosmetic skins and shop backgrounds
  • No ads, no subscriptions, no tracking
  • First purchases came in launch week

This fall · pilot

  • Founding partner shops join free
  • We hand-track every redemption
  • Shops fund only the reward itself

After the pilot

  • Small per-redemption fee, or a flat partner rate
  • Priced under any advertising a shop could buy
  • They pay only for a customer at the counter
For a shop, a redeemed drink is performance marketing priced like a topping.
