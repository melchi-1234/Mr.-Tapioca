Mr. Tapioca field guide 01

The 20-minute phone reset.

For the moment when you sat down to work, touched one notification, and disappeared. No full life overhaul. Just one quiet cup.

1

Pick one app

Choose the app most likely to take the next twenty minutes. Block that one. You are not redesigning your phone tonight.

2

Write one task

Use a finish line you can see: “answer questions 1–8” or “finish lecture 6 notes,” not “study biology.”

3

Set one 20-minute cup

Short enough to start without negotiating. Long enough to get past the first urge to check.

4

Put the phone face down

Let the block screen do its job. If another thought appears, write it beside the task instead of opening a new tab.

5

Finish before you extend

When the cup is full, decide again. Stop, take a break, or make another cup. The first session still counts.
